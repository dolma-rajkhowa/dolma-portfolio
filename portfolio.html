Alienkind: Concept Deck

Instead of a standard product pitch, this visualizes the Alienkind brand as a biological upgrade. The visuals simulate organic textures and fluid glitches to look like leaked clinical research files. It is an exploration of living data, built to feel tactile and organic rather than like a traditional consumer commodity.

AQI Visualiser

This is a real-time environmental visualizer. It pulls live air quality data for Mumbai and uses those numbers to drive the visuals. When the air is safe, the system shifts to green. As the air quality drops, it transitions to blue (moderate) and then red (hazardous). It's a way to see invisible environmental data as something physical and immediate.
