Alienkind Concept Deck

Creative Direction: Continuing the established alien invasion lore, this project reframes the brand through a biological lens. It utilizes generative visuals to simulate organic, bio-scopic textures, acting as leaked research files that position the product as a biological upgrade rather than a standard consumer good.

AQI Visualiser

Technical Logic: A real-time environmental visualizer engineered in TouchDesigner. It connects to a free AQI API to control generative feedback switches, dynamically shifting visual states across green, yellow, blue, and red thresholds. The underlying logic is highly customizable, with the potential to be adapted for localized weather mapping or time-of-day parameters.
